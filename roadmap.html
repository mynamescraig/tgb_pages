GalaxyPaths Classes rework

No more picking one class for life. You build your own character, and you get good at what you actually do.

Led by Spaggy

Build your own path

Spend skill points across Disciplines like Commando, Bounty Hunter, Mandalorian Arsenal or Force Adept. Master two, or mix four or five.

Skill comes from use

Every weapon type has its own track, from Untrained to Legend. Untrained hands are less accurate and hit softer.

Choose your abilities

Disciplines unlock abilities, and you equip four. Controls stay the same F-key combos you use today.

Power through story

The most prestigious powers come from rare Holocrons, server events and staff-run Trials. They can't be bought or handed out by other players.

Spare, arrest or execute

Defeated enemies can surrender. Your choices pull you toward Light or Dark and shift how factions treat you.

Your character's story

A record of your training, teachings, Trials passed and titles earned. Tougher enemies teach you more.

Nobody starts over. Your current class and progress carry across, and old class names like Jedi Guardian live on as titles you earn through your build. Everyone gets one free rebuild after the switch.

GalaxyArmory Weapons rework

A rebuilt arsenal for blasters, lightsabers, grenades, vehicle guns and armor, made to feel familiar on day one and work far more reliably.

Led by Spaggy

Blasters, same feel

Fast flying bolts, scopes and Q to cool off. Magazines become heat, with the same number of shots before you need to cool down.

Better lightsabers

Q ignites and holsters. Deflection is built in, and any two one-handed sabers can be dual-wielded as a stance.

Armor that matters

Armor resists specific damage types like blaster, explosive and ion, and wearing a full set earns a bonus.

Smarter enemies

NPC and vehicle weapons fire real bolts, so a Jedi can deflect a Stormtrooper's shot.

Fixes along the way

Green and yellow bolts become deflectable again, silent NPC attacks start working, and missing saber sounds return.

Safer fights

Friendly fire follows your town and nation, and explosions won't break blocks unless the area allows it.

Keep your gear. Your current weapons and armor convert automatically when you log in, including items in ender chests and shulker boxes. Everything is tested on a separate server before it goes live.
